Method: the unmodified character and one copy per stat, raised by the simulated step, run the same 1000 iterations with the same random seeds. DpS per point is the mean of the per-iteration DpS differences divided by the step (the mean, because damage past an enemy's remaining health is lost, which makes many small differences exactly zero and the median misleading). Values are averages over the step, so a stat near a cap (such as hit) can be worth less than shown, and against low-health enemies, where extra damage pays off in jumps as hits start killing in fewer swings, they can change with the step size. Strength, agility, and intellect are gear points: the sheet's talents scale them, strength adds 2 attack power, agility adds melee crit by level, and intellect adds mana, Mental Dexterity attack power, and Mental Quickness spell power.
